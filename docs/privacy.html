Privacy Policy: Star Eraser for AI Images

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Star Eraser for AI Images ("the extension") is a free Chrome extension that removes the visible sparkle watermark from images you choose. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: nothing leaves your computer.

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing activity, or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts.

Your images

Images you drop into the extension are processed only inside your browser, on your own device. They are never uploaded to any server, including ours. Once you close the tab, the images are gone from the extension; only the clean copies you choose to download are saved, to your own downloads folder.

Permissions

The extension requests no browser permissions. It cannot read your tabs, the websites you visit, or any other data in your browser. It makes no network requests.

Third parties

No data is shared with any third party, because no data is collected.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
